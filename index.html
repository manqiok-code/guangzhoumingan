About Us

Guangzhou Jersey Home International Trade Co., Ltd.

Guangzhou Jersey Home International Trade Co., Ltd. is established in 2024 and based in Guangzhou, China. We are an international trading company focused on soccer jerseys, football kits, teamwear and sports apparel.

Backed by Guangzhou's mature garment supply chain, we integrate sublimation printing, heat transfer, embroidery, cutting and sewing to provide cost-effective football gear and customization services for overseas clubs, fan shops, wholesalers and e-commerce sellers.

📦 Main Products

  • Soccer jerseys / shorts / goalkeeper kits
  • Team training wear / pre-match shirts
  • Retro jerseys / fan version jerseys
  • Custom names, numbers, logos and sponsor prints
  • Basketball jerseys, training T-shirts, sports shorts

✅ Why Choose Us

  • Low MOQ, flexible order quantity
  • OEM / ODM / custom design / sample-based production
  • 100% pre-shipment check on print, stitching and sizing
  • Ship from Guangzhou / Shenzhen by air, sea or express
  • Fast response in English and Chinese

Jersey Home is not a big-brand factory. We are a Guangzhou trading partner who understands football, cross-border business and small-B buyers.

Our principle: stable quality, reliable lead time, transparent pricing.
We welcome global buyers for quotations, sampling and long-term cooperation.
